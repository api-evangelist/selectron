## Software access — gated, not an API

- [Cockpit login / registration](https://cockpit.selectron.ch/de/Account/Login?ReturnUrl=%2Fen%2FDownload)

Cockpit is the customer portal that carries Symphony Suite software packages, system
manuals, product descriptions, firmware, drawings, certificates and catalogs. Access
requires registration with a company email address (private addresses are refused),
manual activation of up to 24 hours, and a software license number. There is no
machine-readable interface behind it and no anonymous download path.
